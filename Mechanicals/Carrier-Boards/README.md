# Mechanical Files for Various Carrier Boards

Drawings and models of various official carrier boards for structural design use.

## Files

- [**DFR1141_Full_EVA_Carrier_Board_for_LattePanda_Mu.zip**](./DFR1141_Full_EVA_Carrier_Board_for_LattePanda_Mu.zip) 3D model of DFR1141 Full EVA Carrier Board, STEP format, without Mu module.
- [**DFR1142_Lite_Carrier_for_LattePanda Mu(V2.0.1).zip**](./DFR1142_Lite_Carrier_for_LattePanda_Mu(V2.0.1).zip): 3D model of DFR1142 Lite Carrier Board V2, STEP format, without Mu module.
- [**DFR1293_Mini_Carrier_Board_for_LattePanda_Mu_and_Mu_Ultra.zip**](./DFR1293_Mini_Carrier_Board_for_LattePanda_Mu_and_Mu_Ultra.zip)  Contains dimension drawings (PDF and DXF formats) and a 3D model (STEP format) of the DFR1293 Mini Carrier Board, without the Mu/Mu Ultra module.
